◆ ShadowForge Explorer
Shielded by design. A Transfer's sender, receiver, and amount are never visible here or anywhere else on the network — only a nullifier and output commitments (opaque hashes) are public. This explorer shows exactly what any stranger can already see from pkg/query's own public API, nothing more, and nothing less. Governance, NFT, and Bank actions are transparent by protocol design, so their real amounts and targets are shown.
